Artwork and copy

The Remotion illustrations run automatically while visible and loop. In the hero, Identity, Audience and Campaign stay visible and connected; Small labels accompany the source and channel pictograms. Fifteen profiles represent the customer base, with four enclosed in a coral contour to show a selected audience. Identity, Audience and Campaign appear below the chain in cyan, coral and green. Each stage grows while the other stages shrink and become grey, before a complete overview closes the fourteen-second loop. Layered surfaces and soft shadows add depth. Store, CRM and Video signals travel into a single customer profile; that same person moves into an audience, which then sends signals to Email, Ads and Video. The pause button in the sticky header controls all animations. The Audience Agent shows three fictional prospect roles: a TV and media audience manager in coral, a retail media manager in green and a brand CRM manager in orange. Each persona is a clickable button, also usable by keyboard. Every six seconds the active example changes automatically. The selected persona first types a request. Only after the request is complete do matching-colored suggested criteria and an illustrative audience size appear. Symbolic portraits regroup in under 250 ms. The example audience sizes are 250 000, 480 000 and 140 000 people, rounded and labelled “example”, all fictional, not product or customer metrics. A click replays the selected request; a paused or reduced-motion experience shows its completed result immediately. Playback holds the selection while a control has focus. People and data are fictional; the illustrations are not production screenshots. Reduced-motion preferences are respected; hidden sections and background tabs stop playback. The Audience Agent demo illustrates a plain-language request and the review of proposed audience rules. The retail demo uses fictional events and a shared loyalty account to illustrate one way to match a click with a purchase. A match records an observed journey. Measuring additional sales would require a comparison. The product team will review the wording and interface before the website launch.

All journeys and demo data are illustrative; they are not screenshots of the production product. An authorised, recent product capture is still needed. Customer stories summarise existing public resources, with no new performance claims.
