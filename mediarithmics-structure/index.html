Artwork and copy

The Audience Agent demo illustrates a plain-language request and the review of proposed audience rules. The retail demo uses fictional events and a shared loyalty account to illustrate one way to match a click with a purchase. A match records an observed journey. Measuring additional sales would require a comparison. The product team will review the wording and interface before the website launch.
