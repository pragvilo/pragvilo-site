From an ad click to an in-store sale.

Step 1 of 3 · Ad click

  1. 01 · Online
    Ad click

    The shopper clicks a campaign ad while signed in to their loyalty account.

  2. 02 · In store
    Store receipt

    The shopper buys in store during the campaign’s measurement window.

  3. 03 · Campaign report
    Matched store sale

    The click and receipt share the same loyalty account. The sale appears in the campaign report.

A matched sale shows an observed journey, not proof that the ad caused the purchase.

Artwork and copy

The Remotion illustrations run automatically while visible and loop. In the hero, Identity, Audience and Campaign stay visible and connected; Small labels accompany the source and channel pictograms. Fifteen profiles represent the customer base, with four enclosed in a coral contour to show a selected audience. Identity, Audience and Campaign appear below the chain in cyan, coral and green. Each stage grows while the other stages shrink and become grey, before a complete overview closes the fourteen-second loop. Layered surfaces and soft shadows add depth. Store, CRM and Video signals travel into a single customer profile; that same person moves into an audience, which then sends signals to Email, Ads and Video. The pause button in the sticky header controls all animations. The Audience Agent highlights each stage automatically while the request, rules and audience remain readable. Choosing a brief updates all three stages; playback pauses while a control has focus. Reduced-motion preferences are respected; hidden sections and background tabs stop playback. The Audience Agent demo illustrates a plain-language request and the review of proposed audience rules. The retail demo uses fictional events and a shared loyalty account to illustrate one way to match a click with a purchase. A match records an observed journey. Measuring additional sales would require a comparison. The product team will review the wording and interface before the website launch.

All journeys and demo data are illustrative; they are not screenshots of the production product. An authorised, recent product capture is still needed. Customer stories summarise existing public resources, with no new performance claims.

Demo request

The three-field form is a local prototype. It validates the input and previews the request, but sends and stores nothing. The production form, recipient and privacy wording need client approval. No calendar or response-time promise is implied.
