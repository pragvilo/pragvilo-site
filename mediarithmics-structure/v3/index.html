Show notes

V3 · Design review

Independent graphic direction by Pragvilo. The V1 and V2 files are unchanged. This is an indicative website mockup, in English, for review.

The direction is deliberately product-led: a working audience example in the first screen, precise rule rows, a connected customer record, and restrained customer stories. Brand colours, DM Sans and the existing customer logos are retained. No generated portraits or unverified performance figures.

The Audience Agent examples and customer record use fictional data and illustrate a workflow. They are not production screenshots. The product team must confirm the final agent name, criteria and available activation destinations. The audience examples use fictional data. Additional examples remain available in these notes without JavaScript.

Customer stories and resources link to existing mediarithmics publications. Channel 4 is presented as a media-partner example, not as a consumer brand. All contact links open the real mediarithmics contact page.

No changes have been made to Webflow. The existing review password screen and search-engine exclusion are retained.
