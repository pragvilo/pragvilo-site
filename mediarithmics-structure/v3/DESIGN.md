# mediarithmics V3

Marketing-site register: upstream frontend-design owns the direction; premium accessibility, layout stability and reduced-motion rules apply.

The user selected the product direction: very sober, precise, close to a well-designed professional tool. The first screen holds a single audience workflow, with a request, explicit criteria rows and its review result. A customer record explains the connected platform. Large decorative artwork was removed. Customer stories use quiet logo covers. No stock or generated portraits, repeated rounded-card grids, arbitrary metrics or decorative numbering.

Runtime tokens live in `v3.css` `:root`: navy #061E44, deep #093058, raised #0A2450, line #1C4378, strong #3A5C8F, primary #EAF1FA, secondary #9FB3CD, light #F7F9FC, light-line #D2DCE8, light-muted #4D5F80, cyan #00A1DF (light-text variant #0077A8), coral #FF5959, green #00AD68, orange #F58D39, violet #7057D1 for agentic content only.

DM Sans local variable font for display/body/utilities. Display 69px, section 43px, body 16px. No shadows or decorative gradients. Rounded buttons 8px, workbench 12px. Interaction transitions at most 200ms with cubic-bezier(.2,0,.2,1); only pointer hover motion. No autoplay or scroll entrance effects. Content is immediately visible.

Native details menus with explicit Escape/outside-click close; ordinary buttons and aria-pressed for example selection; nav toggle controls aria-expanded; focus-visible ring; no synthetic form or fake submit. Notes behind Show notes. Narrow layout reflows at 900px and 600px, without clipping or horizontal overflow. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and forced-colors. Existing password screen is reused, not represented as server-side protection.

Sources: existing brand guide and public case links from V1; no new customer or performance claims. Contact goes to mediarithmics.io/contact. V1/V2 remain untouched.
